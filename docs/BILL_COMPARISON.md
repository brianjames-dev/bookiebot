# Bill comparison

The approved lower ribbon has 32px visible pills, 9px side padding, 8px gaps and current-mode amounts. Invisible vertical hit padding gives each pill a 44px touch region. Tapping highlights its line with the others at 30% opacity; it does not remove bills, change the selected total or open a dialog. All or a second tap restores every line. Native horizontal overflow and horizontal overscroll containment keep the ribbon separate from the page carousel; vertical document scrolling remains native.

The top-right bill-count button opens the existing accessible dialog with visibility switches. Cancel/close discards the draft; Done saves only bill IDs under `bookiebot-bill-comparison:v1:<owner>` in local storage. Choices persist across months, refresh and reopening on that device, with separate owners and an explicit empty selection supported. Unavailable saved IDs are retained for their return; new bills remain off. Clearing site storage resets the selection. No settings are written to sheets.

`billComparisonHistory` adds recorded Rent, fixed Needs bills and utilities from already-read monthly budget rows. Existing `utilityHistory`, budget categories, Spent, Burn Rate and every financial calculation stay scoped as before. History gaps and quarterly omissions retain their existing semantics. Calendar subscriptions and bills without history can appear as current-month-only choices; no price is backfilled into earlier months. Scheduled estimates affect the selected Current/Projected amount under the existing inclusion rule, but never create recorded historical points. A selected series without recorded data says so.

Graph inspection retains exact monthly values. Highlighting limits the tooltip to that bill and shows its positive recorded average before the inspected month and the difference. Current-month due-day annotations never appear on an earlier month's point. The separate Details button/list is removed. Onboarding and manual bill creation remain future work.

All four pages share a body height of `clamp(460px, 56vh, 580px)`, an unchanged 64px sliding title row and 24px bottom padding. This removes 60–80px of excess common height while keeping one stable stage during graph, filter and calendar-mode changes. Category Mix drops its phantom 42px footer row. Burn Rate keeps its 280px plot ceiling, accepted popup placement/lifecycle and compact summary.

Manual acceptance: on a phone, check both themes and Current/Projected; scroll the pills to both ends, highlight and reset, toggle bills in the picker, Cancel, Done and reopen/refresh/change month. Check another user and an empty selection. Swipe the graph outside the ribbon and vertically scroll from the ribbon. Inspect a historical month and a bill with no earlier history. Visit all four graphs, toggle Category Mix callouts and Calendar full month, and inspect Burn Rate nodes near the edges; nothing should clip or change the common section height.
