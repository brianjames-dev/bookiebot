# Bills in Settings

Open **Settings → Bills → Manage bills & subscriptions**. Use **Active bills**, a type filter, or **Ended bills**, or choose **Add bill**.

- **Subscription:** a monthly, quarterly or yearly charge, under Needs or Wants.
- **Variable bill:** the app predicts an unentered amount from past bills. Use **Enter monthly amount** when the actual arrives; it replaces the prediction. An optional configured estimate overrides the historical average.
- **Fixed bill:** a known amount repeating monthly, quarterly or yearly. It follows the saved payment date as an automatic budget expense. Fixed and variable bills support Needs, Wants, Rent and Bills & utilities categories.

Legacy Rent is classified as Fixed even if its older schedule has no automatic-tracking flag. This classification does not change an amount, enroll a schedule, or infer a recurring split.

## Variable bill predictions

Bills and Projected Overview/Calendar mark predictions with a small sparkle icon. Tap it for the amount, history count and month range; Projected Spent uses the same marker in its existing explanation button. Current excludes these variable predictions, and Transactions stays actual-only.

The baseline is the arithmetic mean, rounded to cents, of positive personal bill amounts in available completed months before the selected month. Reads include the annual workbook and its available previous-year history. Blank/zero placeholders, refunds, spreadsheet errors, duplicate sources/periods, current/future months and other predictions do not train the estimate. Quarterly/yearly bills forecast only in their configured billing months. A bill without usable history or a configured estimate stays unknown.

Existing future worksheets also require one verified blank/zero source before showing an estimate. Errors, refunds, missing or ambiguous rows stay unknown. Duplicate checks use the same bill identity across spelling/punctuation aliases, including explicit configured estimates.

The source is your recorded personal budget amount, which may already reflect historical split repayments; it is not a newly quoted full purchase price or a repayment promise. Predictions do not create transactions, change splits, enroll settings or write budget cells. Saved estimates retain their existing explicit override behavior. Enter the actual purchase/payment amount through the normal reviewed monthly-amount flow; its existing split defaults and recorded date remain authoritative. A recorded zero after reimbursement also suppresses the prediction.

Bills and Overview use the same calculation. Opening a prediction explanation preserves an editor draft. Folding, native refresh and editor animations retain their existing behavior; the explanation closes when the view changes. Already-entered amounts retain the normal queued/recorded timing, and never get replaced by an average.

Confirming a variable invoice records its **bill amount**, not a payment. Its prediction disappears and a small clock opens its scheduled details. The saved autopay date controls when that known amount enters Current and Transactions. Projected/Calendar include it on that date beforehand; Transactions stays actual-only regardless of Overview mode. Correct a scheduled invoice through **This month’s bill → Edit amount**, using the existing canonical review/confirmation inside Bills. If the initial split needs recovery, that action becomes **Review split**. The original amount/source/action, split and repayments remain authoritative, including zero personal exposure.

An early reimbursement is a separate cash event: it never marks the payer's utility paid. A fully confirmed recipient repayment retains its real receipt date. November's already confirmed invoice amounts and settled splits are preserved; timing changes do not log them or their repayments again. Automatic posting follows the saved date, without requiring a bank match or a second paid confirmation.

A future recurring change, including a new split, does not hide this month’s confirmed variable-bill amount in the list. If no confirmed amount is available, retain a verified prediction when one exists. **Amount needed** means neither is available; it does not describe the recurring split. The configuration’s Recurring split row shows the saved/drafted split separately.

## Daily amount check-in

On opening/resuming the app, a native **Confirm upcoming bills** prompt groups variable bills still needing an invoice amount in the current prepared billing month. The default window starts seven days before autopay and continues if an amount is overdue. Only the payer is prompted. A known amount, confirmed zero, stopped bill, off-cycle month or unavailable source is excluded. The window begins no earlier than that prepared billing month; opening the app never creates future worksheets or records an estimate.

Choose a bill to open **Bill amount** directly in its existing Bills editor. **Review → Confirm amount** replaces the prediction and keeps the saved autopay date. **Amount check-in** allows 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days before payment, or Off. **Later** dismisses the grouped prompt until the next Pacific day, across that person's devices. No push permission is needed. Active drafts, pending/uncertain changes, text entry, messaging and other dialogs defer the prompt. Abandoned display reservations expire instead of consuming the day.

## Recurring configuration

Set the recurring amount, payment day, frequency and account. **Applies from** defaults to the current month, or the saved upcoming version's month when opening that version. Choose the current month or a later month; the saved recurring rule starts in that exact month and continues until changed again. Selecting November shows **Applies from: November 2026 onward** in the existing review row and saves November, even when a November charge is already recorded. Back, switching rows and folding retain that selection and prepared review. There are no separate selected/recurring start labels.

An eligible unrecorded fixed charge uses the new November amount and appears in November Transactions once its saved payment day arrives. A recurring split starts with that selected month's eligible charges, including an elapsed payment day. Recorded occurrences retain their original amount, category, payment date and split separately from the recurring rule, including fully repaid zero cash expense. They remain one actual transaction rather than being recreated. A managed legacy monthly bill can retain a valid payment date and actual transaction even when its old configuration has no expected price. Variable estimates still never become actual transactions.

Copied future worksheet values do not push the starting month forward; existing nonzero literal amounts and explicitly recorded future charges/allocations stay protected. An unrecorded zero placeholder can receive the reviewed automatic amount. Opening a bill with a scheduled change loads that upcoming configuration; the list shows the configured recurring price rather than this month's paid amount. Quarterly billing uses a first month and repeats every three months for all bill types; day 31 uses the month's last day when needed. Payment day can be cleared and retyped naturally; Review requires a whole day from 1 to 31. **Stop bill** has an independent, backdatable Stop from month; cancelling Stop preserves the configuration month. Its reviewed historical corrections are described below. Restart an ended bill by opening and reviewing its configuration, or use Undo cancellation to restore an unchanged cancellation and its selected charge removals. A missing payment date can be repaired without changing the recorded amount or split.

Type is editable for eligible new charges too. Changing type retains Quarterly; selecting Subscription restricts the category to Needs/Wants and resets an incompatible draft category to Needs. Existing historical subscription categories remain readable. One review lists every changed field with its old and new value: name, type, recurring amount/estimate, frequency/cycle, payment day, category, account, split and restart status, plus the selected Applies from month. Unchanged settings are omitted. Review is read-only, and Confirm becomes available after its slide settles so a rapid repeat tap cannot save. Bills reuses Transactions' merchant/company logos and category fallbacks. In either DUO pose, tap the selected bill to close its editor or another bill to switch directly; drafts and prepared reviews return when selecting the prior bill while browsing. **Add bill** is always an available button, retaining a new draft on repeated taps. During an unresolved save, it queues opening the draft until that original change is verified. Cancel, leaving the workspace or a completed confirmation clears that editing session.

**Recurring split** opens its native controls immediately in both DUO poses, without a second enable checkbox. An unsplit bill starts a By income draft; an existing split keeps its percentages. The circles sit side by side with the income button between them, and the editable values and full-width slider below. **Remove split** returns a No split draft to the configuration; it still requires Review/Confirm. **Use split** returns its draft to the configuration; the bill’s Review/Confirm saves it for eligible current/future charges onward. Cancel restores the prior split. By income is calculated from the household income split when each new charge is recorded. Equal, fronted and custom percentages remain available. Existing charges and repayments are untouched by a recurring default.

Recurring changes follow **Edit bill → Review bill → Confirm → close**. The review offers **Also update November’s charge** only when the selected start is the current month and its recorded amount or split would actually change. Its default is off. Selecting it adds that charge’s amount, split method and both people’s shares to the same review before confirming. A type-only change or future-only start has no current-charge question or extra screen. A variable estimate never replaces a recorded actual. Protected repayments explain why the optional current correction is unavailable without blocking future configuration.

One confirmation drives the existing recurring save and any explicitly included canonical amount/split operations. A due virtual subscription is prepared internally at its original recorded price only when that confirmed scope requires a current correction. There are no preparation, per-operation review or completion screens in the normal flow. Direct **This month’s bill → Edit amount** also combines amount and split into one native editor/review and closes when saved. No repayment is marked received by editing a split.

Keep the selected bill at its original list position through editing, Review, confirmation, recovery and the entire closing animation. A type/filter change cannot move or remove its row during the flow. When the workspace has fully closed, release its retained order/filter anchor and ease any resulting movement once. Open/closed layouts share this behavior, the same reviews and native slide transitions; folding retains the current draft or confirmation.

**Enter monthly amount** remains separate from recurring configuration. Scheduled invoice corrections stay inside Bills; paid transactions remain editable through the existing Transactions flow too.

Settings replaces the BookieBot brand with one connected glass Back/title capsule for **Settings**, **Bills** or **Widget setup**, using Message/Settings’ surface and press feedback. Closed DUO places the configuration editor below the selected bill with the native transaction guide and expansion. Switching rows closes/opens both editors concurrently; each divider stays below its complete editor until closing finishes. Open landscape uses Transactions’ fold, reserved right strip, scrollbar styling/location and pane insets: only the left list scrolls, while the roomier right editor keeps its controls above the dock. Selecting another bill moves opaque surfaces through one stable, clipped pane with standard 440 ms easing. A persistent material and stable compositor layer cover the handoff; rapid switches continue from the visible position, and exited surfaces hide before removal instead of returning to the origin. This avoids exposed overlapping panels or a fade; configuration, recurring split, monthly amount, Stop and current-charge steps retain native 440 ms slides. Edit bill has only its heading and icon-only trash, without repeated merchant/month subtitles. Fields, labels, tinted surfaces, review rows and footer buttons retain Transactions’ styling. The active editor remains mounted through folding, preserving raw invalid percentages and prepared canonical reviews. Reduced motion settles directly.

Open DUO shows Refresh next to Add bill, with a rotating icon while its request runs. Closed DUO uses the existing pull-to-refresh gesture, with no toolbar refresh button. Refresh preserves an editing draft and is unavailable during an unresolved financial operation. The bill filter reuses the actual Category Mix dropdown and always stays enabled, including while refreshing or confirming. A selected bill remains visible as its draft's row anchor even when it does not match the filter.

If a save loses its response, use **Check saved status**. A temporary read/review failure between completed writes offers **Resume saved change**, including after reload, to continue the remaining confirmed steps. A persisted successful receipt is consumed locally; recovery mode cannot be carried into a newly created command. **Retry same confirmation** retains the exact reviewed command and original request ID, including a current-charge confirmation that never reached the server and has no receipt. Each exact operation is retained in an owner-scoped browser journal before it is submitted; successful stages are retained before reading the next source. Each operation still has its own durable server claim and receipt. The whole flow is not one atomic sheet transaction: if a later stage is blocked or fails, the screen identifies the already-saved recurring settings, preserves the last confirmed scope and offers status recovery. Fresh canonical source/version, receipt and exact-share checks precede each later write. An already claimed writer cannot be repeated. Previously confirmed requests from the older multi-screen release remain recoverable. A retained Transactions tab verifies its own pending receipt after Bills settles it, so recovery does not leave a stale navigation guard. Unresolved bill and transaction commands block another financial confirmation for that owner. Reconnecting retains owner-scoped server recovery.

## Ending a bill and correcting missed cancellations

The trash icon opens **Stop bill**. **Stop from September** excludes September and all following billing months; it is the first excluded month, not a service-expiration day. The initial selection keeps the existing safe default when a charge is recorded; the picker now also allows the current month or a past month within configured available workbook history. Backdating is an explicit choice, followed by the charge-removal review. Ordinary recurring edits still use current/future Applies from. Legacy already-confirmed Stop requests retain their original safeguards.

One review lists individual charges from the chosen month, their amounts and whether they will be removed or kept. Verified automatic subscription occurrences and exact automatic fixed-bill formulas start selected. Uncheck any to retain it. Bank-linked charges, Shared allocations, manually entered/recorded payments, and ambiguous historical subtotals stay protected with a short reason. Confirmed future invoices retain their saved autopay dates. Future unentered charges and predictions stop with the recurring rule. A prior month's split cannot protect an unrelated later charge.

Removing a $15 September charge reduces September's spending by $15; October and November corrections likewise belong only to their original months. No refund, reimbursement, income or aggregate credit is posted to the current month. Monthly worksheet cells/subtotals, Current/Projected readers, Transactions and historical graph sources use the same corrected schedule/occurrence data. Deselecting a charge freezes its original amount/date/category independently of the ended rule, so stopping this month need not defer to the next cycle.

The review pages its charge list inside the existing editor rather than adding confirmation screens. Short open DUO shows two rows per page; other layouts show four. All selections share one confirmation. Retain the row/filter anchor until every annual stage and the closing animation finishes. Native slides, folding, inline guides/dividers and independently scrolling left rows stay unchanged.

**Active bills** and the type filters exclude ended definitions. A future-dated end remains visible as **Ends [month]** until its stop month takes effect, then appears under **Ended bills** on refresh. It is archived, not erased from financial history. The archived editor supports a new reviewed restart, changing its ending, and the header's **Undo cancellation** icon when an original cancellation record exists. Undo presents the original-month increases in one review and restores the recorded definition/selected removals. It refuses to overwrite a bill, charge map, source row or corrected subtotal changed since cancellation; use a fresh correction instead. An unsubmitted historical stage has nothing to undo.

Annual books are corrected separately using the existing Bills owner claims and atomic sheet receipts. The current definition/corrections and each older year's changes are individually recoverable, not globally atomic. One confirmed scope freezes every annual command/version and stable request ID. Unknown outcomes check the receipt in their original year; already completed stages never repeat. A read-only server continuation can restore the confirmed review and pending stage on another device. No continuation GET writes data. Next-day historical recovery ignores generated metadata timestamps while retaining source/selection/bank checks. A partial failure identifies saved settings and preserves Check saved status/Resume; a newer cancellation or undo cannot resurrect an older unfinished flow.

The existing hidden **_BookieBot Bill Settings Log** appends an optional `cancellation_audit` column containing this bill's before/after definitions, occurrence maps and corrected cells. All existing leading receipt columns and unknown fields remain compatible. Keep archived definitions and original audit records; remove only explicitly selected automatic occurrence maps, with untouched transactions, repayments and bank records retained. No new financial ledger or database schema is introduced. Missing or changed history is rejected before its writer; all bank schedule caches and owner transaction caches are invalidated after a successful correction.

Schedules remain in each owner's annual budget workbook:

| Hidden tab | Authority |

| `_BookieBot Bill Schedule` | Fixed and variable bill versions, with the existing leading columns preserved |
| `_BookieBot Subscription Schedule` | Subscription versions after the first explicit subscription save |
| `_BookieBot Bill Charges` | Stable plan/month source mapping and recorded occurrence metadata |
| `_BookieBot Bill Settings Log` | Atomic configuration/projection save receipts |

Native settings append stable plan IDs, exclusive effective intervals, type/category, active status, recurring split defaults and version/request metadata. Quarterly subscriptions persist their cycle in a trailing `pull_months` column; old leading columns and legacy yearly `pull_month` reads remain compatible. Each schedule tab enrolls independently on an explicit save. Baselines retain earlier versions. Unknown columns and extra source formulas are preserved; editable names and values are literal cells, never executable formulas.

When a recurring rule starts in a month with a recorded occurrence, the existing Bill Charges table saves its original configuration and payment date in optional trailing `plan_snapshot`, `virtual` and `payment_date` columns. The original leading columns, charge/action/request identities, source cash and reimbursement ledger remain unchanged. This metadata and the recurring rule share the existing atomic configuration receipt. Already-due virtual subscriptions retain their original price once in the summary; explicitly preparing one for editing replaces its virtual metadata with one physical source and removes its summary contribution in the same batch. The review fingerprint also binds whether a current charge has become due, so crossing autopay requires a fresh review.

Changing type retains the plan's original hidden tab, source label and identity; effective `bill_type` metadata determines its classification. A physical monthly source remains physical across later changes. Subscriptions assigned to Rent or Bills & utilities also use monthly sources, preserving their category in budget cells and report readers rather than disappearing from the Needs/Wants subscription summaries.

Converting an automatic bill to Variable clears unrecorded schedule-price formulas from the effective month. Its estimate stays in Projected; literal actual payments and recorded charge/split sources remain unchanged. Month preparation also removes an unrecorded automatic formula copied by an older rollover script for a variable plan.

Before enrollment, the visible `Subscriptions` tab remains the legacy source. After enrollment, the hidden subscription schedule is authoritative and legacy sync cannot overwrite it. The visible tab is retained as the legacy reference. Existing monthly subscription formulas are rebound to the appropriate hidden price versions, with prior calculated values frozen where needed. Reports, Transactions, reminders, widgets and bank schedule reads use effective versions; an empty/stopped managed schedule cannot revive its old visible mirror.

Monthly sources use B for their stable label, C for Needs and D for Wants. New sources append below existing data with named anchors and extend the corresponding subtotal; they do not insert rows through income or transaction history. A permitted category move transfers an existing literal occurrence to its new column without replacing it with the configured price or clearing a variable amount. The recurring rule's new price remains separate. Existing recorded cash cells and confirmed repayments remain authoritative. A physical monthly source suppresses the corresponding virtual subscription occurrence. Confirmed zero cash expense is still recorded and cannot generate another forecast estimate.

Adopting a virtual subscription updates its physical source, charge mapping and that month's subscription summary in one atomic batch. Summary projection excludes recorded plan/month occurrences even after full repayment, while future virtual occurrences remain scheduled. An unenrolled subscription schedule enrolls its complete legacy baseline in that confirmed adoption; Review never enrolls it. An unidentifiable summary blocks adoption before any write.

Summary recovery recognizes an empty amount only in its category's column: C for Needs and D for Wants. A zero in the other column remains untouched.

Ordinary personal bills remain separate between owners. A confirmed repayment for a newly configured shared bill may provision a uniquely identified partner source, such as **Brian · Student Loan**, without overwriting that person's own loan. Existing shared Rent/PG&E/Recology/Water labels remain compatible. Recipient entries are read-only repayment projections and cannot create forecast bills.

Financial changes reuse the canonical action history, split allocation and repayment services. SQL stores operational claims/frozen requests, not the recurring schedule database. Review and Save bind the owner, source snapshot, plan version, optional current-charge choice and exact original/target shares. The existing atomic settings receipt retains the confirmed current target for lost-response recovery; no new financial writer or schedule table is introduced. Bills and Transactions share the existing owner claim lock and block each other's uncertain writes. Source batches also use the existing mutation/projection locks. A source save that cannot finish split/history recording is recovered as recorded and directed to the native transaction editor; its amount is never submitted twice.

Legacy Discord bill amount writers and bank schedule-price mutations cannot bypass managed configuration/history. Enter managed amounts in Bills and edit recorded charges in Transactions. Read-only Discord subscription and spending queries use managed authority.

The background recurring worker runs independently of reminder opt-in. It prepares missing monthly sources and applies explicitly saved split defaults to new due charges once. It never regenerates a cleared existing amount. New months and years are supported by the Apps Script rollover changes and a claimed backend carry-forward/preparation fallback, including installations with an older external script. Charge mappings remain in their original annual book; schedule version history carries forward.

Month/year preparation retains each operational claim. A definitively rejected or pre-write failure gets a new retry identity; running, uncertain and successful attempts retain their identity and cannot repeat the writer. Unknown outcomes still require receipt recovery.

### Why a cancellation month says Keep

Cancellation checkboxes depend on each original month’s source evidence. For a
subscription represented by a category subtotal, the saved monthly subtotal must
match the reconstructed subscription history for that category. A mismatch blocks
automatic removal of any charge in that category/month; the same subscription
can therefore be removable in one month and protected in another. The disabled
row retains its reason and amount. This does not prove the particular
subscription is wrong: an older literal subtotal or another subscription change
can cause the category-wide difference. Verify the original source before
correcting it; stopping future recurrence alone does not erase that amount.

Entered payments, bank-linked charges, Shared allocations and confirmed future
payments have separate protections. Cancellation never bypasses them simply
because another month can be selected.

### Verified subtotal-only repair

A stale aggregate can be repaired without changing individual bills. Reconstruct that exact month/category using effective-dated schedules and preserved virtual charge snapshots; exclude annual charges outside their billing month and exclude adopted physical rows already counted separately. Freeze historical subtotal values. For the current month, reuse the native managed summary formula with its price-version references and frozen-charge amounts so later adoption can maintain it. Re-read all relevant sources immediately before a precise subtotal-only batch, then verify actual amounts, schedule/charge records, formats and validation stayed unchanged. Replay the existing subtotal/cancellation checks; never enable a checkbox by weakening the guard. A subtotal repair does not stop a bill or authorize deleting its charges. Refresh Bills and reopen the review to discard stale source fingerprints.
